Connect the handoffs between apps

A new form enquiry often needs to reach a CRM, an owner and a follow-up queue. A signed agreement may need to start onboarding. A completed task may need to update another application. Those handoffs are good candidates for a focused automation.

Before building, agree the source record, destination fields, identity rules and the outcome you want. Supported app actions, workspace permissions and the account plan determine what can run.

Workflows to scope

  • Lead routing Send an enquiry to the appropriate destination and assign follow-up without creating duplicate records.
  • Onboarding tasks Connect an approved customer event to internal tasks and notifications.
  • Data handoffs Format and map fields between supported business apps or available APIs.
  • AI with review Produce a draft or classification and pause for a person before an important external action.

Review plan limits and exceptions

Check task volume, polling or trigger behavior, available actions and required paid features before committing the scope. Development costs and ongoing app subscriptions are separate parts of the operating budget.

Plan for incomplete records, expired access and a failed downstream step. An owner should know where to review runs and when a retry is safe.

Keep an approval step where it helps

If a workflow prepares a message or changes an important business record, the scope can include a human review before proceeding. The exact action and reviewer access must be checked in your workspace.

Platform reference: Zapier Human in the Loop. We test approval, rejection and missing-information paths as part of the agreed workflow.
