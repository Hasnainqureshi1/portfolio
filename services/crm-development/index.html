A CRM shaped around the work you do

A custom CRM can help when spreadsheets, inboxes and disconnected tools make it hard to see who owns a lead or what happens next. Start by mapping the customer journey, the information staff need and the decisions they make each day.

Before building, compare that scope with configuring an existing CRM. If a standard product covers your process, a focused integration may be a better investment than a new application. Custom development fits workflows that need their own interface, data model or business rules.

What your first release can include

  • Lead and contact management: customer records, enquiries, ownership, search and a clear activity history.
  • Sales pipelines: agreed stages, follow-up tasks and visibility into the next action for each opportunity.
  • Roles and permissions: access rules for staff, managers and administrators, enforced through the application API.
  • Operational dashboards: filters and reports based on definitions your team agrees, such as unassigned leads or overdue follow-ups.
  • Data imports: reviewed field mapping, duplicate handling and a sample migration before moving the full dataset.
  • Integrations: website forms, email or other systems through their supported APIs, subject to account access and provider capabilities.

The first milestone should complete one useful journey: receive an enquiry, assign an owner, record contact and schedule the next step. Additional modules can follow once that journey works for your staff.

Connect your CRM to AI and automation

AI can help classify enquiries, draft a summary or extract agreed fields for staff to review. We define sample inputs, acceptance criteria and fallback behavior before putting those outputs into the workflow.

n8n workflows or Zapier automation can move approved data between systems. WhatsApp integration can support a customer conversation where the available business account and API support it. Failed actions need an owner and a retry path so leads are not silently lost or duplicated.

CRM project questions

How much does custom CRM development cost?

The estimate depends on user roles, screens, workflows, integrations and the condition of existing data. Share those inputs and a budget range so we can scope the first release. Development, hosting, paid APIs and maintenance should be estimated separately.

Can you connect an existing CRM?

We can review the platform's API, account permissions and required data flow before committing to an integration. This service focuses on custom applications and API workflows; platform-specific configuration or specialist certification requirements are assessed separately.

What happens to our existing records?

Start with a sample export and an agreed field map. Review duplicates, required fields and rejected records, then agree backups, a trial import and the cutover plan before moving production data.

Who maintains the application after launch?

Repository access, deployment ownership, operating notes and the support arrangement are agreed in the project scope. Your team should know how to manage users, review failures and request future changes.
