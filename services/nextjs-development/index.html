When this service fits

This service fits teams that want a custom React-based website while keeping a familiar publishing workflow in WordPress. It is relevant when your site needs reusable content sections, strong visual presentation and room for future application features.

Separating the frontend from the CMS adds responsibilities: content models, previews, publishing updates and deployment need to work together. If a conventional WordPress theme already meets your needs, a separate Next.js frontend may add unnecessary maintenance. We should establish the reason for using a headless approach before building it.

What to include in the scope

  • Page and component inventory: the content types, page templates and reusable sections your site needs.
  • Content integration: how CMS fields map to frontend components and how editors publish changes.
  • Responsive implementation: navigation, typography, images and interactions across mobile and desktop.
  • Search foundations: crawlable page content, descriptive metadata, canonical URLs and sitemap coverage.
  • Release checks: content completeness, broken links, form journeys, accessibility and performance observations.

A redesign also needs an inventory of existing URLs before launch. Changing page addresses without mapping their replacements can break visitor journeys and existing links.

Questions before hiring

Can a London or US team hire you remotely?

Yes. I offer remote development from Karachi, Pakistan for London, wider UK and US teams. My portfolio includes frontend work for Cambridge Research Park in the UK and 1540 Broadway in New York. Share your team's time zone, review process and required working-hour overlap so we can establish a suitable schedule for your project or role.

Can you work with our existing WordPress content?

Yes. Start by sharing the current site, content types, plugins that affect the frontend and the pages you want to keep. We can then identify which content can be reused and which fields need a different structure.

Can you build from an existing design?

Yes. Share the design, mobile layouts and interaction requirements. I can connect the page implementation to reusable components and CMS content rather than treating each screen as an isolated page.

Does using Next.js guarantee higher Google rankings?

No. A framework does not guarantee traffic. The site still needs useful content, accessible page URLs and a working visitor experience. Technical implementation supports discovery; search demand and relevance also matter.

Do you also build applications and APIs?

Yes. For authenticated dashboards and product workflows, see my SaaS MVP development service. Recruiters can review my resume and full-stack experience for full-time remote roles.
