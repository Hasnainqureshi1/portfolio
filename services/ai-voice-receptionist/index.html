Design the call before choosing the voice

A receptionist needs to understand the purpose of the call and help the caller reach the right next step. Start with your opening greeting, business hours, common questions, transfer rules and the details your staff need after the call.

Useful first workflows include handling an enquiry, collecting a callback request, answering from approved business information or requesting an appointment. A booking should be confirmed only after the calendar or booking service accepts it.

Connect the conversation to your business

  • Business knowledge Use approved opening hours, locations, services and answers, with an owner for updates.
  • Appointment requests Check the available integration, ask for the required details and make the outcome clear.
  • CRM or team handoff Send a structured summary to the agreed destination and track failed deliveries.
  • Human escalation Define transfer or callback behavior for unsupported requests and conversations that need a person.

Telephony, phone-number availability, speech services and calendar/CRM access are confirmed before the implementation is quoted. Existing numbers and call-routing requirements affect the setup.

Agree behavior for difficult calls

Plan for a caller interrupting, an unclear name, a noisy line, an unavailable calendar and a failed transfer. The assistant should ask for clarification or fall back to a callback route instead of pretending a task succeeded.

We also agree how the assistant introduces itself, what information it should collect, whether any transcript is retained and who can review it. Test with realistic sample calls before switching a live business number.

A practical delivery scope

A first release can include the call script, approved knowledge, one scheduling or CRM integration, a handoff path and a review of test calls. An operator view or integration with n8n can be included where it helps staff follow up.

Platform reference: OpenAI voice-agent architectures. The voice and telephony stack is selected around the actual call workflow; operating costs are estimated separately.

Plan your receptionist

Share the business type, country, existing phone setup, approximate call volume, opening hours, top call reasons and preferred handoff destination. Include the booking system and the actions the assistant may take.
