Before you commission a website

Practical questions.
Straight answers.

A useful brief starts with the property, the people using the site and the content your team needs to manage.

How much does a commercial property website cost?

I quote after reviewing the scope. A single-building website with supplied designs and content requires different work from a campus site with multiple templates, availability feeds and CRM integration. Use the website cost calculator for a planning range, then share your page list, designs and content status with the website brief builder so I can prepare a relevant estimate.

How long will development take?

The timeline depends on the number of templates, design readiness, content, integrations and review rounds. I provide a schedule after reviewing those items. If you have a fixed marketing launch, include that date in the first brief so we can discuss what is realistic.

Can our team update availability and property content?

Yes. We can make agreed fields and sections editable in WordPress, including availability, property details, photography and news. My Cambridge Research Park work connects WordPress content to a Next.js frontend through GraphQL and ACF components. Publishing and editor handover form part of the agreed scope.

Can you work from our agency’s existing designs?

Yes. Share the desktop and mobile layouts, brand guidelines, image assets and interaction requirements. We can review missing states and CMS needs before implementation. The case studies above describe my frontend and content integration contributions.

Does a property website need Next.js and headless WordPress?

No. A conventional WordPress website may be sufficient for a simpler brief. A separate Next.js frontend is worth considering when the presentation, reusable components or application requirements justify the extra publishing and maintenance work. We should choose the setup around your needs.

Can you replace an existing website and retain its page URLs?

We can inventory the existing URLs and keep them where practical. Where addresses change, the release plan should include permanent redirects, updated internal links, canonical URLs and a new sitemap. Search positions can change during a migration, so rankings cannot be guaranteed.

Do you work with UK and US property teams?

Yes. I am based in Karachi, Pakistan and work remotely with UK, US and worldwide teams. My portfolio includes a UK research campus and a New York commercial building website. We agree meeting times, working-hour overlap and review arrangements before starting.
