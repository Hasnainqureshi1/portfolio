WhatsApp automation development

Connect WhatsApp enquiries to a useful business workflow: collect the right details, answer approved questions, route the conversation and keep your team involved. I offer WhatsApp Business integration and automation development for remote UK and US projects.

Start with the customer conversation

Choose one conversation you want to improve: a new enquiry, a booking request, a support question or a status update. Define what starts the workflow, which details are needed and what the customer should receive at the end.

The first useful automation might capture a lead and notify the right team, or gather booking preferences and pass them to a scheduling system. When the question is outside the approved knowledge, the conversation should move to staff.

Messaging and application work

  • Enquiry capture Collect agreed fields and create or update the appropriate record after identity rules are checked.
  • Support routing Categorize a request, attach useful context and assign the follow-up to a person or queue.
  • Knowledge-based answers Use maintained business information or a scoped RAG knowledge assistant.
  • Approved follow-up Connect permitted messages and templates to booking or operational events.

Work with the business account you own

Scoping includes the WhatsApp Business account, phone number, platform/provider access, permitted message types and any template approvals required for the workflow. Your team should own the account and decide who can manage conversations.

Opt-in, opt-out and human handoff belong in the customer journey. Messaging rules and provider charges must be checked for the current account and destination; the integration is designed around those requirements.

Platform reference: WhatsApp Business Platform documentation.

Make failures visible

Incoming events can arrive more than once and downstream systems can be unavailable. Agree how to avoid duplicate records or replies, retry failed work and notify an operator when a step cannot finish.

Test the full conversation and its CRM or calendar result with sample contacts. A delivered message and a completed booking are different outcomes; the workflow should report the outcome it actually knows.

Share your current workflow

Tell me whether you already use WhatsApp Business, which enquiries arrive most often, your CRM or booking system and when a staff member should take over. Include your country, expected message volume and the first workflow you want to launch.
