## Community answer: conflicting CSV contacts

Check whether you have exact duplicate rows or different rows sharing the same customer ID. Keep an unchanged source. If the other fields differ, separate all records with that ID for review before importing; “keep first” can lose the correct address.

Choose the destination's matching rule with its owner. A within-file check cannot tell you which records already exist in the CRM. Test a small import and reconcile created, updated and rejected records.
