A proposed month of work: week 1 measurement and query selection; week 2 proof and internal links; week 3 one validated resource; week 4 relevant distribution and review. This is a work schedule, not a ranking deadline.
5. Track non-brand UK/US clicks to relevant pages, enquiries, qualified leads, and resource-assisted service visits. Preserve the same reporting windows. The goal of 50 relevant visits/month is not equivalent to 50 leads or 50 clients.
