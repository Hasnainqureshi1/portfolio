WORDPRESS ARCHITECTURE DECISION WORKSHEET
Original planning resource by Hasnain Qureshi

1. What visitor task is the existing setup failing to support?
2. Who edits text, images, availability and other changing content?
3. Which page types and content fields are required?
4. Which plugins, forms and integrations must keep working?
5. How will editors preview drafts and verify publishing?
6. Which public URLs must be retained or mapped?
7. Who owns CMS updates, frontend deployment and support?
8. What budget and maintenance capacity are available?
9. Can a simpler theme/template change meet the requirement?
10. What evidence would justify a separate frontend?
