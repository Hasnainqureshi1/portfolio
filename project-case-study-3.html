The New 1540 Broadway

A premium digital showcase for a 900,000 sq ft Midtown Manhattan vertical campus, bringing together the building, hospitality-led amenities, location and available workspace.

ContributionResponsive frontend implementationExperienceCommercial real estate websiteCore stackNext.js · WordPress · Motion

The property story

The website presents the building as a complete workplace experience rather than a list of specifications. Large-format imagery, restrained typography and deliberate motion connect its renovated spaces, amenities, Times Square location and availability.

Frontend focus

The implementation balances visual scale with usable navigation and responsive behaviour. The WordPress-backed content structure allows the building team to maintain key information while the frontend preserves a consistent premium presentation.

  • Responsive Next.js page implementation
  • WordPress-managed content
  • Motion-led transitions and image storytelling
  • Building, amenities, location and gallery journeys
  • Availability paths that remain clear across devices

Experience outcome

Prospective occupiers get one coherent route through the property: first understanding its character, then its workplace offering, location and available space. The design supports exploration without losing the commercial purpose of the site.
