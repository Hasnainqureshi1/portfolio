    About this tool

    Common questions.

    Are my images uploaded or stored?

    No. This page processes files in your browser and keeps them in memory while you use it. Clearing the batch or closing the page removes that working copy. Your original files are unchanged.

    Can I compress several images at once?

    Yes. Choose up to 20 images and optimize the batch with one set of settings. Download each result or a ZIP containing all successfully processed images. ZIP packaging does not further compress the image files.

    Does it support animated images, SVG, HEIC or AVIF?

    The accepted inputs are JPG, PNG and WebP. Animation is not preserved: animated PNG or WebP inputs become a still image. SVG, HEIC, GIF and AVIF inputs are not supported. Browser encoding support determines which output formats are available.

    Does PNG use the quality setting?

    No. PNG output uses lossless encoding of the resized image. Resizing still changes pixel detail. For adjustable lossy compression, choose JPG or WebP.

    Are camera details and colour profiles preserved?

    Do not rely on this tool to preserve original metadata, camera details or colour profiles. The browser decodes and re-encodes the image, so colours and metadata may differ. Keep your originals for print or archival work.
