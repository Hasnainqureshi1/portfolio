About these tools

Common questions.

Each tool page explains exactly what it checks and what it cannot tell you.

Are the tools really free?

Yes. There's no account, limit or trial. Each page has an optional form if you want me to review your results, and you don't need to use it.

What happens to the information I enter?

The cost calculator and brief builder process your answers in your browser. The health check sends the page address to Google PageSpeed Insights. Your contact details and report reach me only if you submit an optional form.

Which tool should I start with?

Budgeting a new website: the cost calculator. Ready to describe the project: the brief builder. Unhappy with a current site's speed or search basics: the health check.
