Cambridge Research Park

An image-led digital destination for a UK science and technology campus, connecting commercial availability with nature, community, events and everyday campus life.

ContributionFrontend and CMS implementationExperienceCorporate campus websiteCore stackNext.js · GraphQL · WordPress

The brief

The site needed to represent more than property availability. It had to show the research park as a connected place to work: buildings and laboratories alongside landscape, tenant community, wellbeing, events and campus services.

Implementation

The frontend combines responsive editorial layouts with content delivered from WordPress through GraphQL. This gives non-technical editors control of the story while the Next.js experience keeps page structure and navigation consistent across devices.

  • Responsive Next.js interface development
  • GraphQL content integration
  • Headless WordPress and editor-managed page content
  • Image-led campus and availability journeys
  • Mobile layouts for long-form property content

Experience outcome

Commercial information and campus storytelling sit inside one discovery journey. Prospective tenants can understand available space while also seeing the environment, community and amenities that define the wider location.
